What this short course covers

Invest in your financial literacy. One hour of reading here will build more wealth than years of conventional wisdom.

Across 14 short lessons and one hour of total reading, you will master the foundational ideas that drive long-term wealth.

This course assumes absolutely nothing. You need zero prior knowledge, no existing investments, and no maths skills beyond percentages. If you have a job, a bank account, and a feeling that you should be doing something smarter with your money, this guide is for you.

Your path to financial clarity

No stock picks or life hacks. Just the core concepts that will rewire how you view money, broken into three phases:

  • The Foundation: Why most financial advice is needlessly complex, what retirement actually means, the real cost of ignoring your money, and how to build a bulletproof cash buffer.
  • The Engine: How compounding actually works, why your pay raises keep disappearing, and the single most important wealth lever you control.
  • The Strategy: Why boring index funds beat clever stock picks, why time in the market wins, and how to calculate your exact financial target.

Mark each lesson complete as you go. It only tracks in your browser, but it unlocks a shortcut into the full course later.

Where this fits. This short course is designed to cover the most valuable financial concepts in the shortest possible time. We also have a completely free 45-lesson course, Halal Money, From Zero, which explores Islamic finance, zakat, and real estate in much greater detail. Both are built for complete beginners, so you can start with whichever suits your needs.

Before we begin

Forget about the financial mistakes you have made in the past. The finance industry thrives on making you feel like you are already too far behind to catch up. You are not. You are exactly where you need to be, and starting today is the only thing that matters. Success does not mean you will become a day trader glued to a screen. True financial success is boring. The goal is to set up a system that runs quietly in the background, protects you from disaster, and grows your wealth automatically while you get on with enjoying your life.

This is general education, not personal financial advice tailored to your unique situation. It does not know your income, your debts, your family, or your tax position. If you need a specific plan built for your exact numbers, consult a qualified financial adviser.
