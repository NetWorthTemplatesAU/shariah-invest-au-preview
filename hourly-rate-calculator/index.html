Real Hourly Rate Calculator

Work out your real hourly rate

Every purchase is traded time. Find your real after-tax rate, then see what the big things actually cost, priced in hours of your working life instead of dollars.

New to this? Why hours, not dollars

You don't earn money, you exchange hours of a finite life for it, at a fixed rate. The number on your payslip isn't your real rate, because the tax portion was never available to spend. Divide what you actually take home by the hours you worked to get it, and you get your real hourly rate: the honest exchange rate between your time and everything you buy.

This is only useful for genuinely big purchases, roughly a week of take-home pay or more, where the decision is infrequent and there's a real choice to make. Pricing a coffee or a subscription in hours doesn't help you and isn't the point here.
